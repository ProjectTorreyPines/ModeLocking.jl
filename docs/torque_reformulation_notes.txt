Torque Reformulation: mu*(Omega_0 - Omega_t) → T_NBI*(1 - Omega_t/Omega_0)
=============================================================================

Status: REVERTED (2026-06-28). Results were incorrect; needs further investigation.
This file documents the intended change for future implementation.


1. MOTIVATION
-------------
The old viscous torque mu*(C1 - Om) has an implicit dependence on C1: the
restoring torque grows linearly with the equilibrium rotation. This means at
high C1 (fast rotation), the plasma is artificially harder to lock.

The new form T_NBI*(1 - Om/(2*pi*C1)) uses the actual NBI torque as a fixed
physical constant. The factor (1 - Om/Omega_0) naturally goes to zero at
equilibrium and the torque magnitude is set by the NBI source, not by an
abstract viscosity coefficient. This is more physical: the NBI beam deposits
a fixed torque regardless of what rotation the control-space sweep assumes.


2. MATHEMATICAL EQUIVALENCE (at operating point only)
-----------------------------------------------------
At the operating point where C1 = Omega_0 (the actual equilibrium rotation):

    mu * (C1 - Om) = mu * C1 * (1 - Om/C1)

So T_NBI = mu * C1_operating. But T_NBI is FIXED for all C1 in the grid,
while mu*C1 varies. The two forms are identical only at C1 = C1_operating;
they differ elsewhere in the grid.

Key difference:
  Old: effective restoring torque = mu * C1 (grows with C1)
  New: effective restoring torque = T_NBI   (constant)
  Effective viscosity: mu_eff = T_NBI / (2*pi*C1)  (varies with C1)


3. NORMALIZATION OF T_NBI
-------------------------
The correct normalization (from the user) is:

    ode_params.nbi_torque = torque_at_rat_surf / (b0^2 / mu_0)

where:
  - torque_at_rat_surf is the NBI torque in N*m at the rational surface
  - b0 is the magnetic perturbation scale (Tesla)
  - mu_0 is the vacuum permeability
  - b0^2/mu_0 has dimensions of energy density * volume ~ energy ~ torque

An earlier (incorrect) attempt used:
    nbi_torque = mu_dim * Omega0_dim
This is dimensionally consistent but just recovers the old form at the
operating point — it doesn't use the physical torque directly.

------------------

f) FUSE locking_actor.jl — set_phys_params!

5. WHY IT WAS REVERTED
-----------------------
The bifurcation boundary and locking probability maps came out wrong after
the switch. Suspected causes (not yet debugged):
  - The bifurcation discriminant derivation may have errors in the mu_eff
    substitution (the algebra is involved for the NL case)
  - The mu_eff = T_NBI/(2*pi*C1) creates a singularity as C1 → 0
    (Control1_min = 0.01 kHz → mu_eff → very large)
  - The normalization of T_NBI may need checking against the ODE energy scale

Next step: reproduce the old results with the old form first, then switch
one piece at a time (RHS only, then bifurcation, then NL) to isolate where
the discrepancy enters.


6. CONVENTION REMINDER
----------------------
With the 2*pi*C1 convention (C1 in frequency units, not angular):
  - Control1_min/max are in kHz
  - Grid: C1_dim = f_kHz * 1e3 * t0  (no 2*pi in conversion)
  - RHS: 2*pi*C1 appears explicitly wherever angular frequency is needed
  - Plots: y-axis shows kHz directly (divide C1_dim by 1e3*t0)
  - ODE state Om is still angular frequency (omega*t0)
